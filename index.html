Orbital Fifths — Quick Songwriter Guide

Circle of Fifths: Outer ring = Major, middle ring = Relative Minor, inner ring = Diminished. The golden window frames the 7 chords naturally in your current key.

Touch & Mouse Controls: Tap any chord wedge to hear its tone and add it to your combo. Long-press on touchscreens (or Shift+Click / Right-Click on desktop) to audition without adding. Tap the outer key-signature bezel anytime to rotate keys.

Narrowing 9,505 Songs: Every chord pressed narrows matching hits from 1900s–2020s across both exact keys and transposed patterns. Tap any green Where Hit Songs Go Next chip to append the most common next chord.

Keyboard Shortcuts: Space Play/Stop • Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y Undo/Redo • Esc Close or Clear • A–L / W–P Play lead synth keys.
